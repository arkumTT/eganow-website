# Photoshoot brief (commission a real shoot)

Real people give the "professional photoshoot" credibility the plan calls for. Use `ASSETS.md` AI prompts for concepting, mood boards and placeholders.

## Project
Eganow brand and website photography. Locations: Accra and Kumasi (2 days) + London (1 day, for diaspora scenes); US/Canada variants can be shot in London with set dressing or commissioned later.
Deliverables: ~16 hero images (P01–P16) + 4 alternates each, edited and colour-matched, plus a clean cut-out of P01.

## Team
Photographer with editorial portrait experience; stylist; hair/makeup; casting director; DIT/assistant; producer for permits and releases.

## Casting (≈24 people)
Mix of ages 20–70, varied skin tones/body types; mothers/grandmothers (P03), young professionals (P01, P04), traders and shop owners (P08, P09, P11, P13), office staff (P10, P12, P14), diaspora adults in London (P02, P16). Real small-business owners where possible. Signed model releases (worldwide, perpetual, web/print/OOH/social/ads), minors only with guardian release.

## Wardrobe & props
Solid white, ice-blue, navy, charcoal; one red accent (scarf, top, card). No visible third-party brands. Props: modern phones (screens blank or green-screen for compositing), a fictional red Eganow card, POS terminal (unbranded), tablet, laptop, produce, shop stock.
Phone screens: shoot with a plain screen; UI is composited later.

## Light & camera
85 mm and 50 mm, f/1.8–f/2.8; soft key (large softbox or window), reflector fill, subtle rim; 5200–5600 K; shoot RAW; also capture each hero setup on a red seamless AND in-location.

## Schedule & budget placeholders
Pre-production 1 wk (casting, locations, releases), shoot 3 days, post 1–2 wks. Budget to be quoted by the producer: photographer, crew, talent fees (buyout), locations, retouching.
