FAQ

Good questions.

What is Eganow Groups?

A transparent and secure way to make group contributions and donations: private groups for friends and family, and public groups for fundraising.

Can I make a group private?

Yes. Group privacy settings let you create public or private groups to control access.

Can I see who has paid?

Yes. Every payment is logged automatically and progress is tracked in real time.

Can I run a Susu group?

Yes. Save together securely with Susu group savings.

Who can contribute to a public group?

Public groups reach a wider audience so anyone can support the cause.
