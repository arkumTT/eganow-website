Account and security

Keep your money and details safe.

How do I keep my account safe?

Never share your PIN, password or one-time codes with anyone. Eganow will never ask for them. Use a phone you control and keep it secure.

Security →
I think someone used my account without my permission.

Contact us immediately. Freeze your card in the app, change your PIN, and keep any messages as evidence.

Tell us what happened through the contact form and include any messages or numbers involved. Never reply to a message that asks for your PIN or codes.
