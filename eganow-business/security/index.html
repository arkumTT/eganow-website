Built to keep your money safe.

Stay safe

A few habits keep you safe.

Eganow will never…

  • ask for your PIN, password or one-time codes
  • ask you to send money to “unlock”, “verify” or “protect” your account
  • ask you to install software or share your screen
  • ask for your full card number or card security code in a call or message

You can always…

  • hang up and contact us through our official channels if you are unsure
  • check who a message is from before you tap any link
  • download the app only from the App Store or Google Play
  • keep your phone locked and its software up to date
  • check every detail of a payment before you confirm it

Scams to watch for

  • Someone pretending to be EganowThey may call, text or message you, often saying there is a problem with your account.
  • A friend or relative in urgent needA hijacked account or new number asks you to send money fast. Call them on a number you know.
  • Prizes, refunds and “fees”If you have to pay to receive money, it is a scam.
  • Fake agents and fake payment linksCheck you are at a real agent point, and that a payment link comes from someone you trust.

For businesses

Keep your business safe too.

  • P

    Protect your API keys

    Keep keys secret. Never put them in public code or in an app that customers can open.

  • C

    Control who has access

    Give each team member their own login with only the access they need, and remove it when they leave.

  • S

    Secure your devices

    Keep POS devices and phones with Egahub safe, and tell us straight away if one is lost.

  • M

    Message responsibly

    Only send bulk messages to people who agreed, and honour every opt-out.

If something goes wrong

Act fast. We’re here to help.

If you think someone has used your account, or you have been tricked, do these four things.

Contact supportMake a complaint
  1. Freeze your card in the app and change your PIN.
  2. Contact us straight away so we can help.
  3. Keep evidence: messages, receipts and numbers involved.
  4. If you are not happy with how it was handled, make a complaint.

FAQ

Good questions.

Will Eganow ever ask for my PIN or password?

No. We will never ask for your PIN, password or one-time codes, by phone, message or in person.

What should I do with a suspicious message?

Do not click links or reply. Contact us through our official channels and tell us what you received.

What if I lose my phone?

Contact us straight away. If you can, freeze your cards from another device and change your PIN.

How do I know a call or message is really from Eganow?

If you are not sure, end the call and contact us yourself using the official details on this website or in the app.
