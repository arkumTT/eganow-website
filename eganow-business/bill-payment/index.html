FAQ

Good questions.

Which bills can I pay?

Airtime and data, electricity and water, media subscriptions, orders and bookings, insurance, pension, taxes and other statutory fees, school fees and memberships.

Can I schedule payments?

Yes. Schedule regular bills once and pay directly from any of your linked accounts.

Which accounts can I link?

Mobile money wallets, international cards and bank accounts.

Do I get a record of my payments?

Yes. The app keeps a record of your bill payments.

Is it secure?

Eganow is licensed by the Bank of Ghana as an Enhanced Payment Service Provider and protects every transaction.
