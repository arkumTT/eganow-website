FAQ

Good questions.

How fast will my money arrive?

Transfers to Africa are completed quickly, often within minutes. The calculator shows the estimated delivery time for your payout method before you send.

What does it cost?

Eganow shows the fee and the exchange rate before you confirm, with no hidden charges. The calculator on this page gives an indicative figure.

Which currencies can I send in?

You can send in your local currency and your recipient receives funds in their local currency. Available currencies are shown in the calculator.

Can I track my transfer?

Yes. Transfers can be tracked at any time in the app.

Is it safe?

Eganow is licensed by the Bank of Ghana as an Enhanced Payment Service Provider and its services comply with regulatory standards.

Where can I read the terms?

Read the money transfer terms and conditions on our website before you send.
