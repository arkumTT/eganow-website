Bill payment · 3 min read

Pay bills on time without the stress: a simple system

Late fees and cut-off notices are avoidable. A small routine does most of the work.

Editorial draft. General guidance; needs editorial and compliance approval before publication.
In this article
  1. List every recurring bill
  2. Pay from one place
  3. Schedule what repeats
  4. Keep your receipts
  5. Review monthly

List every recurring bill

Write down electricity, water, airtime and data, TV and streaming, school fees and memberships, with amounts and due dates.

Pay from one place

Linking your mobile money and bank accounts in one app saves you moving money around just to make a payment.

Schedule what repeats

Set recurring payments for fixed bills so they leave your account on time. Keep a small buffer so a scheduled payment never fails.

Keep your receipts

Digital records of every payment make disputes easy to settle and help at tax time.

Review monthly

Take five minutes each month to check upcoming bills, cancel what you no longer use and adjust amounts.
