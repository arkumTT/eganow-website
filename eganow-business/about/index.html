About eganow

We power happiness.

To become Africa’s leading digital payment and financial services technology platform, empowering individuals, merchants and banks to transact seamlessly across borders.

Who we are

A licensed fintech built for Africa.

Eganow (TekSol Eganow Ltd) is a financial technology company, not a Bank. Eganow is licensed by Bank of Ghana as an Enhanced Payment Service Provider (EPSP), which allows it to offer a wide range of financial services including digital wallets and global payment gateway systems.

  • Licensed and compliant
  • Built to scale
  • Secure by design
  • Africa-focused, globally connected
