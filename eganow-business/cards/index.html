FAQ

Good questions.

What is the eganow card?

A Mastercard you top up from your mobile wallet and use online, in store and worldwide.

Which wallets can I top up from?

MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo mobile money wallets.

Virtual or physical?

You can choose a virtual card for online spending or a physical card for in-person use.

Is it secure?

Cards are protected by PIN, chip and encryption technology, and you can track every transaction.

Where can I use it?

Online, in store and worldwide wherever Mastercard is accepted.
