FAQ

Good questions.

What is Eganow?

Eganow (TekSol Eganow Ltd) is a financial technology company, not a bank, licensed by the Bank of Ghana as an Enhanced Payment Service Provider.

How do I start using Eganow?

Download the app, register an account and link your mobile money or bank account.

What bills can I pay with Eganow?

Airtime and data, electricity and water, media subscriptions, orders and bookings, and insurance, pension, taxes and other fees.

How can Eganow help me run my business?

Accept payments, make payouts, issue cards, run POS devices, serve customers as an agent and send bulk messages, from one platform.

What is Eganow Groups?

A transparent and secure way to make group contributions and donations, privately or publicly.
