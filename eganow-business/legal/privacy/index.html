What we collect

Depending on how you use Eganow, we may collect:

  • Account and transaction details: wallet, account and card references (we show only the last four digits of a card), balances, transactions, recipients and the purpose of payments.

  • Device and usage data: IP address, device type, operating system, app version and logs, and approximate location where you allow it confirm.

  • answer your questions and send important service messages;
  • improve and develop the Services; and
  • send you offers and news where the law allows and, where required, you have agreed. You can opt out at any time.
