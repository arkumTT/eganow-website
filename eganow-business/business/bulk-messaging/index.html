FAQ

Good questions.

What can I send?

Bulk SMS and bulk email to your own contact lists.

Can I see whether messages were delivered?

Yes. Every campaign has a delivery report showing sent, delivered and not delivered.

Can I show my business name as the sender?

Yes. SMS can show your business name as the sender, subject to approval.

Can I schedule messages?

Yes. Send right away or schedule for later.

Can I send from my own app?

Yes. Send from the portal, or trigger messages by API.
