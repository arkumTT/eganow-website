Two services

Cash in. Cash out. Done.

Everything an agent does comes down to these two.

Cash in

Customers deposit cash.

A customer brings cash and wants it in their mobile wallet or bank account. You take the cash and send the money in seconds.

  • The customer hands you the cash
  • You enter their wallet number or account
  • You enter the amount and confirm
  • The money lands in their wallet or account, and they get a confirmation

Cash out

Customers withdraw cash.

A customer wants cash from their wallet or account. You check the request, confirm it and hand over the cash.

  • The customer starts a withdrawal or gives you their details
  • You check the request in the app
  • You confirm the withdrawal
  • You hand over the cash and the customer gets a confirmation

FAQ

Good questions.

What is an agent?

An agent is a shop or business that helps customers put cash into, and take cash out of, their mobile wallet or bank account.

What can my customers do at my agent point?

They can deposit cash into their mobile wallet or bank account (cash in) and withdraw cash from them (cash out).

Which wallets and banks are supported?

We confirm the full list of supported wallets and banks when you apply.

How do agents earn?

Agents earn on the transactions they process. Talk to our team for the details for your business.

Do I need special equipment?

You manage everything from the Eganow app or business portal. We confirm what you need when you apply.
