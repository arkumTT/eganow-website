Virtual and physical

Pick the card that fits the job.

V

Virtual cards

Issued digitally and ready for online spending. Make as many as you need, one for each purpose.

  • Great for ads, subscriptions and online suppliers
  • Create one card per campaign, tool or vendor
  • Freeze or delete a card without touching the others
P

Physical cards

A real card in your team’s hands, for spending in person.

  • Great for field teams, travel and daily spend
  • Same limits and controls as virtual cards
  • Issued to your business or your members

FAQ

Good questions.

What cards can my business issue?

Mastercard virtual cards and Mastercard physical cards, issued from the Eganow portal or by API.

What is the difference between the two programmes?

With team expenses you issue cards for your own organisation. As a re-issuer you offer cards to your members or customers under your own programme.

Who can become a re-issuer?

Businesses and organisations that want to offer cards to their members or customers. Apply and our team will guide you through eligibility and onboarding.

Can I set spending limits and freeze cards?

Yes. You can set how much each card can spend and freeze or unfreeze a card at any time from your dashboard.

Can I issue cards by API?

Yes. Use the Eganow API to create and manage cards from your own app, or use the portal if you prefer not to code.
