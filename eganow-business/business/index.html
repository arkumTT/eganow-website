FAQ

Good questions.

What is Eganow Business?

A payments and digital financial services platform for businesses: accept payments, make payouts, issue cards, run POS devices, serve customers as an agent and message them in bulk.

How do I start?

Open a business account on the Eganow portal and get verified. Our team helps you with the services you need.

Can I use more than one service?

Yes. Most businesses combine them, such as EgaPOS at the counter and BizCollect for online payments and payment links.

Is there an app?

Yes. Egahub is the Eganow Business app for your phone.

Can developers integrate?

Yes. Ask for sandbox access and test before you go live.
