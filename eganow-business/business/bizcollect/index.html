FAQ

Good questions.

Which payment methods can my customers use?

Customers can pay with mobile money, cards and bank transfers. The exact networks and banks available are confirmed when you open your account.

Do I need a website to use BizCollect?

No. You can share a Payment Link or a USSD code without a website. If you do have a website or app, use Hosted Checkout or the API.

How long until I get my money?

You can follow every payment on your dashboard as it happens. Settlement timing depends on your account setup and is confirmed during onboarding.

Can I pay out money too?

Yes. Send payouts to suppliers, staff or customers from your dashboard, one at a time or in bulk.

Is there a sandbox to test in?

Yes. Ask for sandbox access and test with test money before going live.
