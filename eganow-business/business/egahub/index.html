FAQ

Good questions.

Which phones does Egahub run on?

Egahub is available for Android. Other platforms are confirmed on request.

Do I need a separate account?

No. Use the same business account you use on the Eganow portal.

Can I do everything the portal does?

The app covers collecting, payouts, POS, cards, agency banking and messaging. Some account setup tasks stay on the portal.

How do I get the app?

Open a business account, then download Egahub and sign in.
