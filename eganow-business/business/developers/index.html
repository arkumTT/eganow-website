How it works

Ask. Get told. Deliver.

Your system asks Eganow to collect a payment. When the customer pays, Eganow tells your system, and you deliver the order.

  • Your server sends a request
  • The customer approves on their phone
  • Eganow sends your server a confirmation
POST /v1/collections { "amount": 250.00, "currency": "GHS", "method": "mobile_money", "reference": "ORDER-1042", "callback_url": "https://yourshop.com/hook" }
POST https://yourshop.com/hook { "reference": "ORDER-1042", "status": "paid", "amount": 250.00 }

These samples only show the shape of an integration. Real endpoints, fields, keys and security checks are in your sandbox documentation.

Good habits

Build it safely.

  • Keep your keys private

    Never put a secret key in a mobile app or in your website’s public code. Keep it on your server.

  • Wait for confirmation

    Treat a payment as paid only when Eganow tells you it is. Do not rely on the customer’s screen.

  • Check before you deliver

    Match the amount and reference to the order before you hand over goods or service.

  • Expect the odd retry

    Networks fail. Make your system safe to receive the same message twice.

  • Test the unhappy paths

    Try a declined payment, a customer who never approves and a slow network before you go live.

  • Keep a record

    Log each request and reference you send, so you can trace any payment in minutes.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is there a sandbox?

Yes. Ask for sandbox access and our team sets you up so you can test with test money before going live.

Where is the documentation?

The API documentation is at https://docs.sandbox.egacoreapi.com/ and is shared with your sandbox access.

Which languages can I use?

It is a standard web API, so you can call it from any language that can make secure web requests.

Do I need a business account first?

Not to start testing. You need an approved business account to go live.

Who do I talk to if I get stuck?

Use the contact form and choose the sandbox topic. Our team will help.
