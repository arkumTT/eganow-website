FAQ

Good questions.

How do I get a POS device?

Apply for an Eganow business account and tell us what you need. Once you’re verified, Eganow issues and sets up your device.

Which payments can I accept?

You can accept the payment methods Eganow supports on the device. We confirm the full list when you apply.

Can I manage more than one terminal?

Yes. The POS management platform shows every device, branch and sale in one place.

What if a device is lost or stops working?

Contact our support team and we’ll help you get back to selling.

Can I use EgaPOS with BizCollect?

Yes. Many businesses use POS at the counter and BizCollect for online payments, payment links and USSD.
