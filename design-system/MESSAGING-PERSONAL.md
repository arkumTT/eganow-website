# Personal services: messaging review and recommendations

Scope: Home (Personal sections), Money Transfer (+ UK/US/Canada share its copy), Bill Payment, Cards, Groups.
Frame: **Simple. Speed. Secure.** Every headline should lead with one of the three and name the benefit, then the proof sits underneath.
Status: recommendations only. Nothing here is applied to the site yet.

## 1. What the current copy does (review)

| Finding | Where | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines describe the feature, not the win | "Group contributions & donations made easier, secure & transparent", "Move money from mobile money to card" | The reader has to work out the benefit. "money" appears three times in the Cards headline. |
| Corporate filler in leads | "Eganow offers you a secure mobile solution with competitive rates", "an array of recurring services" | Sounds like every remittance brand. No number, no moment, no person. |
| Generic adverbs and clichés | "Instantly. Globally. Seamlessly.", "across the globe", "The game changer", "Experience the convenience... at your fingertips" | Easy to skim past. None is specific to Eganow. |
| The 3S strip is a label, not a message | Hero strip "Fast / Transparent / Secure" | Three words with no proof. Same strip on every page, so it carries no information. |
| Strong lines are buried | "Tired of being locked out of international payments?", "No contribution, no chop", "Mobile money, now global", "Pay like a boss" | These already have personality. Promote them to headlines. |
| Repeated or overlapping copy | Cards H1 = Home slider H2; Bill Payment lists "Investments & fees" and "Government fees" with near-identical text | Wastes prime space; weakens search snippets. |
| Claims without evidence | "Join thousands of satisfied users", "Instantly", "in seconds" | Compliance risk and trust risk (see section 6). |
| Calls to action are mostly "Get the app" | Everywhere | One verb fits all. Tie each CTA to the job: Send, Pay, Top up, Start. |

## 2. Simple / Speed / Secure: reusable lines

Use these as the proof strip, section kickers and app-store style one-liners. Each pairs a promise with a concrete detail.

| Pillar | Short line | Supporting detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | One app. Every account. | Link MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo, bank and cards in one place. |
| **Simple** | See the price before you pay. | Rate and fee shown up front, no hidden charges. |
| **Speed** | Money that moves when you do. | Transfers often arrive within minutes. |
| **Speed** | Set it once. Never be late. | Schedule bills and pay from any linked account. |
| **Secure** | Licensed. Protected. Tracked. | Bank of Ghana licensed EPSP; encrypted; every transaction on record. |
| **Secure** | Know where your money is. | Track transfers and see every payment as it happens. |

Replacement for the hero strip (`Fast · Transparent · Secure`): **Simple to use · Quick to arrive · Safe to trust**, or keep the three words and add a proof under each on hover/tap.

## 6. Claims to confirm before they go live
These are worth getting right because they are the strongest "sell" lines and the easiest to challenge.
- "Within minutes" for transfers (current copy says "often within minutes"). Keep the qualifier unless every corridor and payout method meets it.
- "Instantly" / "in seconds" for card top-ups (the draft says "Instantly"; confirm timing per wallet).
- "Join thousands of users" / "thousands send, pay and save": needs a current verified user count and date, or drop it.
- "No hidden charges", "Rate locked": confirm with compliance and product that these match the terms.
- Boss Card perks and "elite discounts": needs the actual partner list.
- All lines that mention the Bank of Ghana licence must keep the approved regulatory wording.

## 7. Style rules for the rewrite
- Headline: 3 to 7 words, a verb or a result, no more than one idea. Read it aloud: if it needs a breath, cut it.
- Lead with the reader's outcome ("Never be late"), then the feature ("Schedule once").
- Use real names and places (MTN, ECG, Susu, Jumia), not "services" and "platforms".
- Numbers beat adjectives: "fee shown first" beats "competitive fees".
- One Simple/Speed/Secure pillar per section so the three build up across the page instead of repeating.
- Keep the warm, Africa-first voice already in the brand ("No contribution, no chop"). Avoid slang that a compliance reviewer can't defend.
